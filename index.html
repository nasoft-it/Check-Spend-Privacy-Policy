The short version

  • You don't need an account or to sign in to use Check Spend.
  • The expenses, budgets and settings you enter are saved on your device, not on NASOFT servers.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Check Spend (the "app"), a personal finance tool published by NASOFT ("NASOFT", "we", "us"). Check Spend helps you track expenses, organize them into categories, set budgets, and see your spending totals by day, month and year.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Information We Collect

NASOFT does not collect personal information from you. The app does, however, ask you to type in some information so it can work. That information is kept on your device and is not sent to us:

What you enterWhy the app needs it
Expenses: category, name, amount, date, time, payment method (card, cash, bank transfer, digital wallet or other), merchant and notesTo list your spending, show totals and build the charts
Budgets: period budgets and monthly category budgetsTo compare what you plan to spend with what you spend
Preferences: default currency and light/dark themeTo show the app the way you like it

The payment method is only a label you pick for your own records. Check Spend never asks for card numbers, bank account numbers, passwords or PINs, and it does not connect to your bank.

The app has no sign-up or login, so we do not collect your name, email address or phone number. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts for any feature.

How We Use Information

The information you enter is used only inside the app, on your device, to give you the features you asked for: showing your expenses, calculating totals, applying filters and sorting, drawing analytics, and tracking your budgets. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Data Storage

Check Spend is designed to work offline. Everything you add is saved automatically on the device you use the app on.

Delivery of the app

The app's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (nasoft-it.github.io/Check-Spend). When the app loads, your device requests those pages from GitHub, and GitHub may receive technical details that any website visitor sends, such as your IP address and browser type. This is handled under GitHub's own privacy statement. Your expense entries are not part of that request.

Backups you create

The app includes tools to save a copy of your data as a file and to restore from one. Those files are created and kept by you, in a location you choose. NASOFT never sees them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data, because we don't have it. Your expenses, budgets and settings stay on your device unless you yourself copy or send a backup file somewhere.

Third-Party Services

Check Spend does not include advertising services, analytics or tracking tools, crash-reporting services, social media plug-ins, or push notification services. It does not use cookies to track you.

The only outside service involved is GitHub Pages, which hosts the app's files, as described under Data Storage. The Android version of the app displays the same hosted pages inside the app.

Security

Because your records stay on your device, their safety depends largely on the security of that device. We recommend using a screen lock, keeping your device up to date, and keeping any backup files somewhere private. The app is loaded over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

NASOFT holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain. On your device, your entries stay until you delete them, clear all data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Backup files you made stay wherever you saved them until you remove them.

Data Deletion

You are in control of your data and can delete it in any of these ways:

  • Single entries: remove individual expenses inside the app.
  • Everything at once: open Settings, go to Data, and choose Clear all data.
  • Device level: clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Backup files: delete any backup files you saved. We can't delete these for you because we never receive them.

Since we do not hold any of your information, there is no account or server record for us to erase.

Children's Privacy

Check Spend is a general budgeting tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the app changes or the law requires it. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will make that clear in the app or on this page. Please check back from time to time.
