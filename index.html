1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Check Spend ("Check Spend", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and operated by Nasoft, handles information when you use our website at checkspend.dpdns.org and our app (together, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Check Spend does not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number in order to use the Service.

We do not operate our own user database or our own analytics or tracking system. Any information you enter into the Service is used to provide the Service's features to you.

Some information may be collected automatically by third-party services that we use, such as advertising providers and hosting infrastructure. This may include technical data like device type, operating system, IP address, browser type, and advertising identifiers. See sections 4, 5 and 6 for details.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
  • Display advertisements that help support the Service;
  • Keep the Service secure and working properly; and
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and mobile advertising identifiers. Check Spend itself does not use cookies to track you across other websites or apps.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may use cookies, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
